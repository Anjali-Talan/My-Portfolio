About Me

Analyst by training, translator by habit.

Most of my work sits between the people who ask the questions and the data that answers them.

I'm a Data / Business Analyst with ~2 years of experience in requirements gathering, financial and operational data analysis, and dashboard development using SQL, Power BI and Advanced Excel.

Day to day that means sitting with investment advisors, finance leads or operations managers, working out what decision they're actually trying to make, and then building the reporting that supports it — validated, reconciled and repeatable, not a one-off spreadsheet.

I've done that across BFSI, D2C and manufacturing: equity research reporting at Yes Securities, budget and variance analysis at Dreamtech Electronics, and operations MIS at Magicpin. The through-line is the same — translate a business requirement into accurate, decision-ready reporting.
