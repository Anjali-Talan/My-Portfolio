About Me

Numbers are the easy part. The decision is the job.

Most of my work sits between the people who ask the questions and the data that answers them.

I'm a Data / Business Analyst with ~2 years of experience across requirements gathering, financial and operational data analysis, and dashboard development using SQL, Power BI and Advanced Excel - with Tableau and R rounding out the analysis and reporting stack.

Most days that mean sitting with investment teams, finance leads or operations managers and working out what they're actually trying to decide — then turning that into a BRD/FRD, an As-Is/To-Be map, or a gap analysis before a single dashboard gets built. What comes out the other end is validated, reconciled, and built to be reused, not a one-off pull.

I've done that across BFSI, retail-tech and manufacturing: investment data and equity research reporting at Yes Securities, business operations MIS at Magicpin, and budget and variance analysis at Dreamtech Electronics. My first analytics role was at MeriSkill, validating data with Python and Power BI.
